# Content Plan Prompt

```text
Act as the content strategist for this AI-first blog.
Read site/site.yaml, site/taxonomy.yaml and site/voice.md.
Create a 90-day content plan with:
- 4 content pillars — these become `pillars` in site/taxonomy.yaml, and every
  pillar must end up owning at least one topic or the build fails
- 3 series ideas
- 20 article titles with search intent
- 5 video ideas
- 5 project/case-study ideas
- internal link strategy
- SEO/GEO notes for each pillar

Do not create files until I approve the plan. Once approved, the plan lands in
site/taxonomy.yaml — it is not a separate document, so it cannot drift from the
site it describes.
```
